# Buying Signals (ranked strongest first)

A signal = an observable event that suggests the company needs automation **now**.
Only strength + recency move the score. A single weak signal alone is not enough to contact someone.

| # | Signal | Where we see it (cost) | Stays relevant | Strength | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Job post that asks for automation/AI/n8n/Zapier/Power Automate skills in a non-tech company | Indeed UAE (free) | 30 days | **Strong** | They already want automation and are trying to hire for it — we're faster and cheaper than a hire |
| 2 | Hiring **2+** data-entry / admin / back-office / documentation roles at once | Indeed UAE (free) | 30 days | **Strong** | Lots of manual work, growing — classic automation case |
| 3 | Recruitment agency hiring recruiters / sourcers / "CV screening" roles | Indeed UAE (free) | 30 days | **Strong** | Exact match to the HR CV pipeline proof |
| 4 | Single data-entry / admin / operations coordinator post | Indeed UAE (free) | 21 days | Medium | Manual work exists, but one hire may be normal turnover |
| 5 | New Operations / HR head joined in last 90 days | Lusha contact signals (credits — skip on $0) | 90 days | Medium | New leaders want quick wins; not used on free plan |
| 6 | New branch / expansion / funding news | Company website or news (free, only if easy) | 60 days | Medium | Growth → more process load |
| 7 | Job post mentions Excel-heavy reporting, "MIS", "tracking sheets" | Indeed UAE (free) | 30 days | Weak | Suggests manual reporting; needs another signal |
