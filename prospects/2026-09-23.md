## Lessons from this run
1. Lusha's free data is thin on small UAE companies: 1 of 2 not found, the other had no email.
2. Search should require `existing_data_points: ["emails"]` (or check `canReveal`) so credits aren't spent on people with no email.
3. Always look up the company's website domain first and search Lusha by `companyDomains` — name search matched junk.
4. Small UAE firms often publish a real contact email in their own job post / website (Akkad: info@pestcontrol.ae). Proposed fallback — needs Shibil's OK.
