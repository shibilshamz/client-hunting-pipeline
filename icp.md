## Anti-profile (exclude)

- Government, semi-government, free-zone authorities, schools/universities (slow procurement)
- Large enterprises >500 staff and multinationals' UAE branches (decisions made abroad; want big vendors)
- IT services / software / automation agencies (competitors, build it themselves)
- Companies with no website or no clear business domain (can't verify, can't personalise)
- Anyone in `do-not-contact.md` or already in `prospects.md`

## What we believe about "why they buy" [GUESS]
They are paying people to move data by hand (CVs, forms, invoices, listings, patient/lead records) and
they are growing — so they're about to hire more people for it. Automation is cheaper than another hire.
